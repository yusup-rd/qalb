## Using Qalb

Qalb provides Quran reading and related tools, prayer-time calculations, Qibla direction, nearby mosque and route information, Islamic calendar events, zakat metal prices, tasbih, and local prayer notifications. Use the app for personal information and convenience. It is not a religious authority and does not replace qualified religious guidance.

The app is intended to remain free to use. Donations are optional; the current app does not process donations or unlock paid features. Core functionality is not conditional on payment.

## Accuracy and availability

Prayer times and Qibla direction depend on device location, date, selected calculation settings, device sensors, and the underlying calculation method. Check that location and settings are appropriate for your circumstances. Compass readings can be affected by device sensors and surroundings. Nearby mosque records, routes, calendar data, and metals prices come from external services and may be incomplete, delayed, inaccurate, or unavailable. Confirm important information independently.

Qalb and third-party services may change, be interrupted, or become unavailable. The app may be updated, and features or external integrations may change. We will describe material changes in the app or its public materials where appropriate.
