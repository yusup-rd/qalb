## What Qalb provides

Qalb offers Quran reading and related tools, prayer-time calculations, Qibla direction, nearby mosque information and driving estimates, Islamic calendar events, gold and silver prices for the zakat calculator, tasbih, and local prayer reminders. It is for personal information and convenience, not a religious authority or a substitute for guidance from a qualified scholar or local authority.

Qalb is currently free. The current version has no paid features and does not process donations, payments, or purchases; there is no way to donate through the app.

## Accuracy and availability

Prayer times are calculated on your device and depend on your location, date, calculation method, and settings. They may differ from those used by a local mosque or religious authority. Qibla direction depends on location and, when using the compass, device sensors and surroundings. Check the settings and sensor readings where accuracy matters.

Mosque listings and route estimates depend on third-party geographic data and routing services. Calendar dates and events may vary with local practice or observation. Gold and silver prices may be delayed, stale, or different from prices available to you; they are estimates for a calculator, not a valuation or a religious ruling about zakat. Check important information independently and seek qualified religious or financial guidance when needed.

Qalb and third-party services may change, be interrupted, or become unavailable. Features and external integrations may be changed or discontinued.
